Process

How I approach problems.

01

Learn the work first

I shadow the team and map how the work actually gets done before I touch any tools.

02

Pick the right size of solution

Sometimes it's an agent, sometimes an automation, and sometimes a good prompt. If a task takes 30 minutes by hand and won't repeat, it doesn't need an agent.

03

Build small, show early

A rough version in front of the team finds problems a plan can't.

04

Check the output, then get people using it

Someone reviews anything client-facing. I measure time saved and quality, and I run workshops so teams adopt it.
