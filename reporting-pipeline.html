Workflow Automation · Agent

Reporting Pipeline Automation

A workflow and Q&A agent that automated the data prep behind an audience analysis methodology.

Built at Weber Shandwick. Client details are not shared on this page.

01

The problem

Weber uses a methodology that ranks an audience's passion points by volume, sentiment, and engagement.

Behind every report was manual data prep. Someone pulled data from social listening tools, decided what to pull, extracted it, cleaned it, normalized it into an Excel template, then analyzed it and packaged the insights.

It took hours each time, and nobody had flagged it as something to fix.

02

What I built

Most of those steps repeated, so I built a workflow on Vertex AI that extracts, cleans, and normalizes the data and fills the existing template.

On top of it I built an agent that analysts can ask questions at any stage.

I had a working prototype before I brought it to my manager, and I showed it on a real report the team already knew.

04

What stayed with people

I automated the data prep and left the methodology and the analysts' judgment as they were.

Analysts still review the output, rank the passion points, and package the insights.

05

A problem I had to fix

Early on, outputs varied on real data. I traced it to sources that weren't normalized.

I added a cleaning and normalization layer before the data reached the agent, plus validation checks, and results became consistent.

The lesson was that clean inputs matter more than a clever prompt.

06

Result

Got adopted and used across four teams.

07

Limits

The workflow handles the repeatable prep. It doesn't replace the analysts' judgment, and a person reviews the output before it goes anywhere.
